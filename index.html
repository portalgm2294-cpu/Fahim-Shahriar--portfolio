About

What I actually do

Most of my work starts with something raw — a batch of unedited photos, a folder of clips, or just a business name — and ends with something people notice: a clean edit, a punchy short-form cut, or a logo that still reads clearly at thumbnail size.

I split my time between three things: retouching and colour-correcting photos, editing and grading video for reels and shorts, and building brand marks from scratch.
